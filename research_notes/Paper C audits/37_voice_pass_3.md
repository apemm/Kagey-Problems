# Voice pass 3: sec_secondorder.tex

Low-level prose pass, no mathematics, labels, refs or citations changed. About 36 small edits:
- many "So ..." sentences that follow from the previous one became "Hence ...";
- "The proof is in Appendix" became "We prove this in Appendix" or "We give the proof in Appendix";
- "Also" became "Note that" where it introduced a side fact; the filler "This is a forward statement about the face maxima" was cut.

Examples (old, then new):
1. "The objects depend on $A_F$ and $\mu$ only, so a field does not change them." -> "Hence the objects depend on $A_F$ and $\mu$ only, and a field does not change them."
2. "The proof of (a) is in Appendix~\ref{app:torus} and uses Lemma~\ref{lem:perron}." -> "We prove (a) in Appendix~\ref{app:torus}, using Lemma~\ref{lem:perron}."
3. "The constants ... depend on $Q$ and $\mu$. We make them uniform ... This is a forward statement about the face maxima, and the companion paper uses it to ..." -> "... The companion paper uses this statement to ..."
