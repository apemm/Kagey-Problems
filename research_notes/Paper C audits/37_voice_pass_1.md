# Voice pass 1, sec_intro.tex

Small prose edits only. No mathematics, refs, citations or arxivbuild branches touched.
Changes: "So" or "This gives" opening a consequence became "Hence" (5 places), and "Say that" became "We say that".

Examples (old -> new):
1. "So each dimension of the face costs a factor $N$" -> "Hence each dimension of the face costs a factor $N$"
2. "This gives the power $p_F=(k^*-1)/2+|F|-k^*$." -> "Hence the power is $p_F=(k^*-1)/2+|F|-k^*$."
3. "Say that a face $F$ satisfies (H)" -> "We say that a face $F$ satisfies (H)"
