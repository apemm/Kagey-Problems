Theorem 4.6 proves the centred-interval classification in Remark 2.1(b) of
your paper. For each superdiffusive memory, the bimodal biases form one open
interval centred at one half, and the endpoint densities are unimodal. The
proof uses a finite first-difference bound and reflection dominance on the
half favoured by the initial bias, followed by a weak-convergence argument.

Theorem 4.4 also identifies a unique fair-start modal transition near
0.6688128600146. Its proof combines the shape dichotomy with an interval
certificate for the memory derivative of the central curvature and a negative
fourth derivative at the transition. Section 5 separates the formal proofs
from the written analytic estimates and numerical enclosures.
