The rectangle game

Black out some points of a grid. A rectangle with its corners on the grid, tilted ones included, is then shown to you only by the corners you left visible. How many points can you black out so that every rectangle can still be told apart from every other?

The answer is known for 2, 3 and 4 rows and for a few small grids, and it is open beyond that. The proofs and the table of values are in the paper.

The game needs JavaScript. You choose a set of grid points to black out. Then a rectangle is chosen in secret, you are shown only the corners of it that you left visible, and you have to say which rectangle it is.

Your best valid blackout on each grid size is kept in this browser only.
