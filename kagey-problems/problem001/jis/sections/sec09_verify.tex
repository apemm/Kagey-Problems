\section{Computations and verification}\label{sec:verify}

Each computed value and count comes from two separately written programs, except the $7\times8$
count, where one enumeration found $18$ optima and a second did not finish. Code, data, and
commands are in the repository~\cite{repo}.

An ILP program with CBC~\cite{cbc} finds a smallest hitting set for the sets of
Lemma~\ref{lem:diff}, and brute force checks it. SAT encoding A forbids each minimal symmetric
difference of two rectangles. Encoding B has a clause for each pair of rectangles with a common
corner and allows one hidden rectangle. Column searches add columns from left to right, using
$f(6,6)\le12$ on the $6\times7$ and $6\times8$ grids and $f(6,7)\le13$ on the $6\times8$ and
$7\times7$ grids.

CaDiCaL~\cite{biere2024} gives LRAT certificates for $f(5,6)<12$, $f(5,7)<13$, $f(6,6)<13$,
$f(6,7)<14$, $f(7,7)<15$, $f(6,8)<16$, $f(7,8)<17$, and the $70$ instances of
Theorem~\ref{thm:sixrow}(a). Two separate generators give the clauses, and
\texttt{lrat-check} of drat-trim~\cite{wetzler2014,cruzfilipe2017} and the Lean LRAT checker
accept the certificates. The encodings are argued faithful in the repository, not formally.

Lean~4 proves Lemmas~\ref{lem:diff} and~\ref{lem:threecorners}, Corollary~\ref{cor:hitting},
Theorems~\ref{thm:strip}, \ref{thm:rowbound} (both orientations), and~\ref{thm:collar}, and that a
valid blackout hides at most one rectangle. The kernel checks that $S_0$, $S_8$, the blackout of
Theorem~\ref{thm:refute}, the $6\times15$ blackout, and the $5\times13$ and $6\times23$ test
boards are valid, and that $\{(1,2),\allowbreak(2,1),\allowbreak(3,2),\allowbreak(2,3)\}$ hides
$D(1,1)$ on the $4\times3$ grid, so Theorem~\ref{thm:hidden} needs $m\ge4$. Hence Lean proves
$f(5,m)=m+6$ for $m\ge8$, $f(6,m)=m+9$ for $m\ge15$, $f(5,5)\ge10$, and $f(6,8)\ge15$, from the
three standard Lean axioms alone. It found no error. Before it, Theorems~\ref{thm:rowbound} and~\ref{thm:collar}
had only hand proofs. Lemma~\ref{lem:leftmost}, Theorem~\ref{thm:equality}, $f(5,5)\le10$, and
most of Sections~\ref{sec:three} to~\ref{app:square} are not formalized.
