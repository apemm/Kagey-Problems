\section*{A note on the use of AI}

I began this work in August and wrote the original draft and its mathematics myself, together
with most of the Python code. I later used AI tools for editorial revision and proofreading,
including suggestions for revisions and corrections to typos and spelling errors.

I used Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) to draw the figures in the style of Kagey's posting of the
problem. I also used it to check my Python code, and it wrote
further checks of the computer-assisted results. One of these, the script
\texttt{six\_row\_collar\_6x9\_check.py}, gives the lower bound in the second open question of
Section~\ref{sec:open}. It also wrote the Lean files, the checked certificates for the SAT bounds
and the formal checks of Section~\ref{sec:verify}. The formalization did not change any
statement of the paper, and it found no error or missing hypothesis. It changed how several
results are established, as Section~\ref{sec:verify} describes. Finally, I used it to search the
literature beyond the sources I had found, and several references come from that search. I read
every source that the paper cites, together with the parts that support each citation. I checked
every change, and I take full responsibility for the contents of the paper.
