\section{Certificates}

Two independently written generators produce the same CNF clauses for the cell
instances and the $70$ structured instances with $m+9$ points. We split $6\times8$ by its first column into $64$ cases,
and $7\times7$ and $7\times8$ into $128$ each. The second generator checks that these splits
exhaust the first-column choices. The $345$ instances with $m+8$ points on six rows, one for
each labeled graph of Lemma~\ref{P-lem:sixbelow} at widths $9$, $10$, and $11$, are written
by \nolinkurl{jis/scripts/six_row_excess8_export_cnf.py}. Encoding B independently finds all $345$ cases unsatisfiable. The hashes of the CNF files, the
proofs, and the results of both proof checkers are in
\nolinkurl{jis/data/unsat_certificates.json} and
\nolinkurl{jis/data/six_row_excess8_certificates.json}. The proofs themselves are too large
for the repository.

\section{Formal checks}

Kernel-checked validity tests cover $S_0$, $S_8$, the construction
in Theorem~\ref{P-thm:refute}, the $6\times15$ construction in
Theorem~\ref{P-thm:sixrow}, and the $5\times13$ and $6\times23$ collar
boards. The formal proofs also show that at most one rectangle can be hidden.
The kernel also checks that
$\{(1,2),(2,1),(3,2),(2,3)\}$ hides
$D(1,1)$ on $4\times3$. Thus the restriction $m\ge4$ in
Theorem~\ref{P-thm:hidden} is necessary.

The remaining unformalized parts are Lemma~\ref{P-lem:leftmost},
Theorem~\ref{P-thm:equality}, Lemma~\ref{P-lem:sixbelow}, $f(5,5)\le10$,
Lemma~\ref{P-lem:enc}, the seven-row constructions, and the other results in
Sections~\ref{P-sec:strip} to~\ref{P-sec:square}, except Section~\ref{P-sec:general}, which is
formalized in full (\nolinkurl{GeneralLocal.lean} and \nolinkurl{GeneralTheorem.lean}).
