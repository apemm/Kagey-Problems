# Independent audit of protected-domain wall pressure

9 November 2026. The proof in
[Protected-domain wall pressure](protected_domain_wall_pressure.md)
passes an independent audit.

For comparable primitive directions and side scales divisible by four,
all corners retain one coordinate residue modulo four. The horizontal
translation interval placing only the extreme corner across the wall
has length \(\min(ka,\ell b)=\Theta(h)\).
Distinct matching laws disagree on at least half the completion
residues, and the balanced protected row law allows a positive
fraction of vertical translations. Each dyadic direction range
therefore contributes \(\Omega(h^4)\) witnesses. The repeated-column
condition contributes only \(O(h)\) scale/direction choices per range,
compared with \(\Omega(h^2)\) retained choices.

A fixed black point is in \(O(h^2\log h)\) comparable-direction
witnesses. Two fixed points in distinct columns have only \(O(h)\)
possible third points, since the relevant perpendicular lines are
nonhorizontal and circles have at most two points per row.
The resulting conditional masses are \(O(\log h)\) and \(O(1)\).
Independent marking at rate \(b/\log h\) makes these bounds small
while retaining total mean \(\Omega(h)\) at each wall.

The within-domain bulk families have exactly the product of the
individual domain partition functions. After that bulk is conditioned
away, the conditional local-lemma argument supplies the stated
\(\exp(-ch)\) penalty for each wall. The assumed domain lengths keep
every required black support in its old domain and every completion
in the next one.

This is a comparison with exact finite-domain partition functions.
The presently available boundary factors are larger than the wall
penalty, so the note correctly does not infer a Perron comparison,
general entropy capture, or component dominance from this estimate.
