# Independent audit of finite protected-law islands

9 November 2026. [finite_protected_island_pressure.md](finite_protected_island_pressure.md) passes review. The reference count is the exact normalized fixed-final-F partition function, and an island must use a different matched protected law.

At geometric scale d, comparable primitive directions and side scales give Theta(d² log d) shapes. Vertical translation has Theta(h) choices and translation across the selected wall has Theta(d) choices. Three prescribed black letters therefore have mean Theta(d³ log d/h²). One fixed black cell removes both translations and leaves O(d² log d) shapes; two fixed black cells leave O(d) completions in the local range. The marking b/[1+(d/h)² log d] makes the raw and conditional neighbor sums small and leaves the stated W_h(d) mean.

Choosing d to be a sufficiently small constant times min(ell,h) places all three black corners in the preceding base-phase region and the completion inside the island. The other island boundary is outside these supports. Distinct matched laws disagree on a positive fraction of residues modulo four, so a fixed fraction of completions are genuine wrong-class singleton rivals. The completion column does not contribute a variable to the three-letter event: every letter allowed by its actual phase supplies the required final-F rival.

The retained old bulk avoids all changed phase columns, so its joint product law is unchanged. The exact conditional estimate applies before old-bulk restoration. Restoration and alphabet-size costs are additive in the number of changed columns, and are bounded by C ell log⁴h/h. They are absorbed uniformly for h/log^A h<=ell<=h, for every fixed A. For h<=ell<=c h²/log⁴h, a sufficiently small absolute c absorbs the same cost into the order-h wall penalty.

The packet theorem covers smaller lengths. The overlap at ell=h/log³h has wall penalty of order h/log⁸h, so there is no missing length range. Every nontrivial island up to the stated cutoff has relative cost at most exp(-c log²h).

For several long islands separated by sufficiently long order-h base gaps, the chosen left-wall families have disjoint black-variable sets. Consequently their joint incidence has no factor equal to the number of islands. Restoring old bulk costs the sum of the individual restoration costs, which are absorbed separately. The remaining polynomial number of lengths and finite number of island laws are dominated by exp(-c h/log⁸h) per island. Allowing every column as a possible start is an upper bound on the permitted descriptions.

The result does not cover longer islands, arbitrary phase patterns within long islands, or a semi-infinite wall. The assertion about arbitrary short islands retains its full-memory collar assumption.
