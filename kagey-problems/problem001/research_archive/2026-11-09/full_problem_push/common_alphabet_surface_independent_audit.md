# Independent audit of the controlled interface comparison

9 November 2026. [continuation_common_alphabet_surface.md](continuation_common_alphabet_surface.md) passes review. Its conclusion concerns the fixed quadratic matched phase on the common alphabet with all changed rows removed. It does not compare restored black letters or arbitrary phase walls.

On that common alphabet, selected black rows have the same color in both partitions. Hidden and tilt/tilt supports, and the directly occupied horizontal or oblique axis rivals, are unchanged. The protected rectangle identity rules out old singleton and vertical-pair rivals. A newly forbidden singleton therefore completes on a changed row with the old selected color; a newly forbidden vertical pair has exactly one changed endpoint. This gives precisely the T and V families. These events remain forbidden even if another displayed point in the midpoint column is black: the rival then becomes a singleton presentation instead of a two-white presentation.

The containment removals are essential and sufficient. T containing V is redundant, and a bulk event containing either smaller retained support is redundant in the new model. After those removals, distinct events cannot share all assignments of the smaller event. The previous four/six extension table, the O(r h^(1+eta)) one-black-point row-root count, the O(r) two-point row-root count, and the squarefree-kernel O(r sqrt(h)) vertical count give the stated conditional pressure. With eta fixed below 1/2, the r h^(-1+eta) term is absorbed by r/sqrt(h). Uniform conditional probabilities p(1+O(theta)) then follow from the upper conditional local lemma and the lower compatible-neighbor union bound.

Each interface is evaluated as crossing avoidance conditional on its own internal event family. Therefore the comparison has an error proportional to crossing weight, not to the volume of the two halves. The old crossing weight is O(h log²h). For one fixed completion row, primitive-direction summation weighted by horizontal span gives O(h⁴): the k,l sums contribute h³(1/a²+1/b²), and summing a,b up to h gives this bound. Thus the full T crossing mean is O(rh). The V crossing mean is smaller.

The explicit long-angle family has Theta(h²) choices at each changed row, loses only O(rh) choices when other changed rows are removed, and has Theta(h²) cut translations. All coordinate differences are divisible by four, preserving the quadratic phase. The short black pair has irrational length 4 sqrt(1+b²), and the other two black pairs have horizontal gap of order h², so no vertical-rival pair is contained in it. Its probability is of order h^(-3), giving a surviving crossing mean of order rh. The three black corners determine their completion, and the uniquely short horizontal side determines the parameters, so the multiplicity is bounded.

For removed bulk events, the normalized T count O(r h² log²h), normalized V count O_eta(r h^(1+eta)), fixed-support extension counts, and O(h²) maximum crossing span give O_eta(r h^(2eta) log²h). This bound may overcount descriptions and still suffices. It is o(rh) for a fixed small eta. The conditional crossing errors are also o(rh), uniformly for 1<=r<=h^(1/4).

Consequently the new interface factor is smaller by Theta(rh), with the stated uniform sign. The result rules out an absolute O(r log²h) surface bound in this controlled model. It leaves open a bound on positive interface improvement in the full alphabet and in interfaces between different matched phases.
