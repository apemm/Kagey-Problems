# Independent audit of the linear-list obstruction

9 November 2026. The theorem in [linear_slot_filter_obstruction.md](linear_slot_filter_obstruction.md) passes the following checks. It rules out a Cartesian prefilter that leaves a linear number of slots per label and promises safety for every P-and-level-proper selection. It does not rule out coordinated choices from those lists.

The argument first restricts to a low odd-row interval and a high even-row interval. There are Theta(h^2) labels there. Lists of size at least delta h inside a universe of at most K h colors give one color a positive density of eligible labels. Partitioning into fixed D-by-D boxes loses only O(Dh) points. Choosing h after D therefore gives a fixed box with positive density.

Within this box, horizontal differences are bounded by 4D, while every odd/even vertical difference is at least h/2. Once 16D^2<h+1, every nonzero horizontal P test lies strictly between successive squares. This proves independence even in the stronger P graph. It uses distinct odd rows, which are enforced in the next step.

The lattice generated by (1,r) and (r,-4) has fixed index r^2+4. Some coset retains a positive fraction of the eligible box. The inverse coordinate map puts that coset in an O_r(D)-by-O_r(D) integer box. Padding gives a dense bipartite graph, so the elementary two-stage Cauchy--Schwarz count supplies Omega(D^4) rectangles.

For a linear form with two nonzero coefficients, a repeated corner value forces one linear relation between the two side increments. There are O(D) such increment pairs and O(D^2) translations, hence only O(D^3) exceptions. This applies to the odd-row index and to every fixed requested level b+ka. The choice r>10+max|k| makes all relevant coefficients nonzero. Choosing D large enough leaves a rectangle with four distinct odd rows and distinct levels.

The physical vectors (4,2r) and (4r,-8) are perpendicular. Halving the first side gives two integral, nondegenerate rectangles whose symmetric difference is exactly the four eligible even points. Their common points lie inside the original rectangle, hence inside the height strip and horizontal board. Every subsequent filling preserves equality of their white presentations. No assumption about the common points remaining white is needed.

The obstruction itself permits any fixed integer level slopes. The extension to a complete proper coloring uses [global_P_linear_list_coloring.md](global_P_linear_list_coloring.md), whose present hypothesis excludes the slope -1. Under that hypothesis and lists of size at least C_K h+1, precolor the four labels with their common color and delete that color from all remaining lists. The list theorem colors the remaining induced graph. This retains the collision in a full P-and-level-proper placement.

The width and scope conclusions are consistent: independent per-label permissions alone cannot enforce all required right-angle exclusions in O(h) blocks. A multi-label coloring rule, adaptive restrictions, or a different coordinate map remains possible. The separate calculation in [global_P_even_angle_reduction.md](global_P_even_angle_reduction.md) also shows why controlling only within-block even chords does not solve the cross-block case.
