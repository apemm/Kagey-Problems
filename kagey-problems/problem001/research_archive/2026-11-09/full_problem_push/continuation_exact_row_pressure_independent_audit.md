# Independent audit of the continued exact row comparisons

9 November 2026. [continuation_exact_row_local_cap.md](continuation_exact_row_local_cap.md) and [continuation_domain_witness_frequency.md](continuation_domain_witness_frequency.md) pass review with the stationary-measure scope clarified below. They give exact comparisons for restricted phase languages and a necessary witness frequency, not full canonical or partition dominance.

The reference family must use normalized fixed-final-F supports throughout, including witnesses with a common white corner outside the currently counted black-support interval. A globally valid sea word obeys these necessary constraints. The retained canonical bulk avoids changed rows in its entire geometry and avoids phase-error columns in its required black support. On every other column, the common allowed letters agree and all changed-row letters are invisible to that bulk. Their dummy multiplicities differ by only O(1), since actual and reference class sizes are h/2+O(1). Conditional unary exclusion therefore gives the stated exp(Cm/h) alphabet comparison in both directions.

For a quadratic reference, every matched phase law is nonconstant and every changed row is eligible in half of its period-four positions. Thus the adaptive row-root family with R of order sqrt(h/r) has period-averaged mean P=(r/h)log²(h/r), including r=1. Its removed-row fraction is small uniformly for r<=delta_0 h, and its one-/two-assignment extension pressure is O(sqrt(r/h)log(h/r)+r/h). The finite endpoint loss O(sqrt(rh)) is harmless in the interval estimate. Removing one phase-error column costs CP raw mean by bounded-period translation counting, rather than the larger conditional extension bound.

The adaptive phase roots lose only O(r/h) of their vertical translations when all geometry on changed rows is removed. Their completion rows lie outside the changed set, whereas row-root completions lie inside it. Three prescribed noncollinear corners have a unique completion, so the two families cannot have a distinct common three-assignment support. Their mixed one-/two-assignment bounds are therefore the existing separate extension bounds. Small fixed epsilon_0 and delta_0 make the unthinned joint conditional comparison valid.

The full reference restoration cost r h^(-2+eta)+h^(-1) is o(P), uniformly over 1<=r<=delta_0 h for fixed eta<1. The error-column cost log⁴h/h and the CP loss are absorbed by the phase-root weight. The adaptive block activity is exponentially small in log²h and is negligible even relative to the one-row penalty. This proves the exact local-cap comparison without an extensive protected-bulk approximation error.

For one constant-law interval of M h-blocks, the one-sided roots use the longer available direction and the common length cap log M. Their variables remain in that interval. The capped activity bound has total logarithmic cost at most M exp(−c log²h)+Ch M exp(−c log²M); the second term is bounded by C'h uniformly in M. Row endpoints cost O(sqrt(rh))<=O(h). Combining this with the audited exact protected cut factor exp(Ch log²h) gives the claimed interval estimate. The black-support convention is the same on both sides, so no finite-prefix boundary equivalence is being assumed.

The witness decomposition uses O(hK)+O_h(1) guard columns and at most K+1 good constant-law intervals. Counting guard words directly introduces no separate phase entropy. Possible witness descriptions contribute at most binom(2 ceil(m/h),K) C^K. For K/m<=cP/(h log²h), both the interval boundary factors and description entropy are absorbed into a fixed fraction of the row penalty. The O(1/h) exact quadratic-variant difference is smaller than the minimum P=log²h/h. Hence this low-witness language is strictly below the balanced quadratic protected rate.

The stationary consequence applies to a Perron measure of a competing irreducible finite-state component and each of its cyclic h-step pieces, or more generally to an h-step ergodic piece whose own entropy is at least the reference rate. An arbitrary stationary mixture need not give its total entropy to each ergodic piece. With this qualification, the necessary witness frequency of order r/h² in the optimizer neighborhood follows from the word-count bound. It does not assert that every word of the component has that frequency.
