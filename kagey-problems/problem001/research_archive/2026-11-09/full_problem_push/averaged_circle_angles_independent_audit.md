# Independent audit of averaged circle angles

9 November 2026. [averaged_circle_angles.md](averaged_circle_angles.md) passes independent mathematical and source review. It removes the nearly horizontal contribution at the h³log⁴h scale. It does not establish the remaining primitive parity-type asymptotic.

The two cited inputs apply as stated. Theorem 5 in [Fainsilber, Kurlberg and Wennberg](https://kurlberg.github.io/eprints/circle2.pdf), printed page 10, gives the reciprocal-prime absolute-cosine estimate with coefficient 1/π for a fixed nonzero frequency divisible by four. Taking the frequency to be 8|j| meets its hypothesis once the prime cutoff is large enough. Lemma 2.18 in [Matomäki, Radziwiłł, Shao, Tao and Teräväinen](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00222-026-01408-6) states Shiu's bound under the displayed prime-power and subpower hypotheses. The constructed function is bounded by d₃, so these growth constants are uniform in both N and j.

The local angular majorant is correct. At split primes outside N it uses the exact absolute coefficient at exponent 2e. At split primes dividing N, the inequality ν+2e+1≤(ν+1)(2e+1) avoids division by an angular coefficient that might vanish. The prime two and even inert-prime exponents contribute absolute value one. The exceptional-prime factor is bounded by exp(CΣ_{p|N}1/p), a power of 1+log log N. Combining the cosine estimate with Shiu gives the sufficient exponent 2/π<1. One may apply the prime-sum estimate on each dyadic interval before summing, which directly yields the stated summatory bound.

For N polynomially bounded and K polynomially long in h, division by the previously proved constant-coefficient mean leaves (log K)^(2/π−1) times a fixed power of log log h. This tends uniformly to zero. A fixed finite Fourier approximation to a fixed arc therefore suffices; the Fourier cutoff is sent to infinity only after h. Rotational symmetry correctly eliminates frequencies not divisible by four. There is no assertion about shrinking arcs on individual circles.

The horizontal split is also sound. When k≤h/(εa), the dyadic primitive-box bound contributes a factor B/A. Summing B≤A/log h and then A gives O_ε(h³log²h). For ka>h/ε, strip containment forces the alternative diagonal into the fixed arcs |sin θ|≤ε. The aggregate arc estimate controls each long scale interval by O(ε)+o(1) times the full-circle majorant. Enlarging partially used intervals has bounded overlap and does not change its O(h³log⁴h) total bound.

The audited short-scale truncation costs O(h³log³h+δ²h³log⁴h). Taking h to infinity first, then δ and ε to zero, proves the claimed o(h³log⁴h) horizontal contribution. The order of these limits keeps every angular estimate within its stated fixed-arc, fixed-frequency range.
