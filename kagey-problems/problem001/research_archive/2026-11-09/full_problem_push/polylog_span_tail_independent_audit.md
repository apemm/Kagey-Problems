# Independent audit of the polylogarithmic span tail

9 November 2026. [polylogarithmic_span_tail.md](polylogarithmic_span_tail.md) passes review, including its common-strip comparison and quantitative optimizer consequence. The argument does not require a convergence rate for circle-angle or primitive-weight asymptotics.

For an omitted aspect ratio b/a>h/P, one has a<P and b>ha/P. The harmonic sum over this range is O(log²(2P)); the reverse range is identical. Thus the omitted rectangle degree at any corner is O(h² log²(2P)). Pairing an omitted rectangle with any rectangle through the same corner gives six-support mean O(log²h log²(2P)/h).

For the mixed count, the fixed-coordinate hyperbola estimate is uniform. Primitivity makes a invertible modulo every divisor of a²+b², so the residue count is bounded by the number of roots of minus one. Summing that root count and its reciprocal-weighted version gives F_a(B)<=C[B log(2(a+B))+a+B]. In the vertical tail, b is much larger than a; dyadic summation then gives O(log h log³(2P)). Interchanging coordinates in the horizontal tail gives O(log²h log²(2P)). The circle-scale inequality and its first moment turn these into the claimed mixed-support tail. This is an upper bound and may include otherwise inadmissible circle representations.

The retained primitive aspect ratio bounds the side and perpendicular-connector horizontal spans by O(h²/P). An alternative equal-norm diagonal has horizontal component at most the side's horizontal component plus h. Hence the complete mixed witness, and the union of two retained rectangles sharing a corner, obey the same span bound. Hidden and horizontal mixed supports have lower total mean and may be omitted. Restricting to the original minimal support family does not increase the omission bound.

Taking P=log⁶h gives omitted mean O(log²h(1+loglog h)²/h) and support span O(h²/log⁶h). Its cut cost is O(h/log²h), so the fixed witness deficit in the full-shift proof absorbs it.

The exact quadratic-reference comparison is valid in both directions. On either height-h strip, unary exclusion of its extra boundary letter has probability O(1/h) per column and satisfies the joint local lemma with the protected bulk. The conditional product bound loses at most exp(Cm/h). On the resulting common alphabet, use the deduplicated canonical hidden/mixed/genuine-six family. Same-side or same-diagonal witnesses containing a common hidden support add no new event and must not be counted with arbitrary displayed-pair multiplicity. The remaining differing witnesses touch one of the two boundary rows, so their total weight is O_eta(m h^(-2+eta)). The union local lemma gives the two explicit comparisons through Z_U. Consequently the exact quadratic variants differ by O(1/h) in logarithmic growth, including the slightly unbalanced translated reference.

Replacing the earlier power-span truncation by this explicit tail leaves the near-protected row penalty unchanged and makes the remaining error O(log²h(1+loglog h)²/h). The global theorem already puts every optimizer near a quadratic reference. Comparing that reference with the balanced quadratic lower bound yields r log²(h/r)<=C log²h(1+loglog h)². Monotonicity for r<h/e² gives r=O((1+loglog h)²).

Dropping the negative row penalty also yields 0<=R_h-r_Q(h)<=C log²h(1+loglog h)², where r_Q is the exact protected quadratic rate. The same upper and lower sandwich applies to the fixed-height maximum-blackout exponential limsup. These estimates do not establish exact quadratic optimality, a unique maximal component, or exact counting prefactors.
