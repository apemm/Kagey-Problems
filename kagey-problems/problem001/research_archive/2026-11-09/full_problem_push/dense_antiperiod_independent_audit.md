9 November 2026. This note independently checks two ingredients for
the unknown-partition entropy-stability argument. Neither ingredient
assumes a fixed column period.

The complete written theorem in near_maximal_partition_rigidity.md
also passes independent review. In particular, every fixed positive
distance from all six protected row laws forces a fixed positive
entropy gap below \(h/2\), uniformly over balanced row partitions
for sufficiently large \(h\). The review includes conditional block
entropy, extraction of the dense lag sets, robust bounded-period

## 1. A stationary process with many approximate antiperiods

Let \(T=(T_i)_{i\in\mathbb Z}\) be a stationary binary process.
Suppose \(D\subseteq[1,H]\cap\mathbb Z\), \(|D|\ge\delta H\), and
\[
 \Pr(T_0=T_d)\le\epsilon\qquad(d\in D).
\]
For fixed \(\delta>0\) and sufficiently large \(H\), there is an
integer \(g\le 2/\delta\) such that
\[
 \Pr(T_0\ne T_{jg})\le C_\delta\epsilon
 \qquad (|jg|\le H).
 \tag{1}
\]
Consequently a length-\(H\) sample is within expected Hamming distance
\(C_\delta\epsilon H\) of a \(g\)-periodic word.

Here is a proof that avoids iterating an approximate one-step period.
Write \(d=|D|\), \(l=\max D-\min D\), and
\[
 g=\gcd(D-D),\qquad m=l/g.
\]
The trivial case \(m=1\) is included below. The normalized set
\((D-\min D)/g\) has \(d\) distinct integers in \([0,m]\).
Thus \(g\le l/(d-1)\le2/\delta\), and \(l\ge d-1\).

Consider the Cayley graph on \(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z\) generated by
\((D-D)/g\). It is connected because the integer gcd is one.
Each closed neighborhood has at least \(d-1\) elements: the two
endpoints of the normalized set coincide modulo \(m\), and its
other elements remain distinct. On a shortest path, closed
neighborhoods of every third vertex are disjoint. Its diameter is
therefore at most \(3m/(d-1)+2\).

Lift a shortest path to integer generators in \([-m,m]\).
Its sum differs from the desired representative by at most a
bounded number of multiples of \(m\). Since both \(m\) and \(-m\)
belong to the generating set, these wraps can be corrected using
at most the path length plus one further generators. Hence every
multiple of \(g\) in \([-l,l]\) is a sum of \(O_\delta(1)\)
differences from \(D-D\).

Stationarity and a union bound give
\[
 \Pr(T_0\ne T_{d_1-d_2})\le2\epsilon
 \qquad(d_1,d_2\in D).
\]
The bounded representation above proves (1) for \(|jg|\le l\).
Since \(l\ge\delta H-1\), any remaining multiple with magnitude
at most \(H\) is a sum of \(O_\delta(1)\) such shifts. This proves
(1) on its stated range.

For the Hamming conclusion, repeat the first \(g\) sample bits
periodically. Each comparison uses a shift covered by (1), so
linearity of expectation proves the claim. No long chain of
one-step errors is summed.

## 2. Exact pressure from simple vertical diamonds

Fix a balanced row coloring \(c:[h]\to\{0,1\}\), with classes
\(B_0,B_1\). Define
\[
 n_s(k)=\#\{a\in B_s:1\le a-k<a+k\le h,\
                         c(a-k)\ne c(a+k)\}.
\]
For a fixed phase word \(t\), choose independent row letters
uniformly in \(B_{t_x}\). Whenever \(t_x=t_{x+2k}=s\), each row
counted by \(n_s(k)\) gives the forbidden assignment
\[
 (x,a),\quad(x+2k,a).
\]
These are the black horizontal diagonal of the tilted diamond
whose other two corners are
\[
 (x+k,a-k),\quad(x+k,a+k).
\]
The latter row pair is an edge of the full final bipartite graph.
If neither displayed point is selected at the middle column, this
is a vertical pair rival. If one is selected, the remaining
singleton still has the corresponding rival. The assignment is
therefore a valid positive certificate independent of the
middle-column letter.

Deduplicate by the two required black cells. Each event has
probability \(|B_s|^{-2}\). A column belongs to \(O(h^2)\) events,
so its total incident probability is \(O(1)\). After conditioning
on a specified black cell, there are only \(O(h)\) compatible
events, each with remaining probability \(O(1/h)\). Distinct
events sharing both required cells do not remain after
deduplication.

Attach independent Bernoulli markers of a sufficiently small
absolute probability. Both the raw and conditional neighbor
probability sums are then uniformly small. The product-form local
lemma, including its arbitrary-event conditional bound, gives a
fixed positive lower bound times the original probability for
each marked event after any earlier event subset has been avoided.
Multiplying the successive avoidance probabilities yields
\[
 \#\{\text{valid words with phase }t\}
 \le \prod_x |B_{t_x}|\,
 \exp\!\left[
  -\frac{c}{h^2}
  \sum_{\substack{x,k\\x+2k\text{ in the word}}}
  n_{t_x}(k)\,1_{\{t_x=t_{x+2k}\}}
 \right].
 \tag{2}
\]
The marked avoidance event contains actual validity, which is the
correct direction for this upper bound. The full final row graph
is part of the hypothesis.

## 3. The entropy consequence of the two ingredients

For a stationary valid sea process with phase process \(T\), (2)
and conditional block entropy give
\[
 h(\text{row process})
 \le h(T)+\log(h/2)+O(1/h)
 -\frac{c}{h^2}\sum_{s,k}n_s(k)
                         \Pr(T_0=T_{2k}=s).
 \tag{3}
\]
Finite-end losses disappear when block length tends to infinity
at fixed \(h\).

Suppose the row coloring stays a fixed positive distance from
ordinary parity. The proved reflection-stability theorem gives
\(\sum_k n_s(k)\ge c_\delta h^2\) for both \(s\). If the left side
of (3) approaches \(\log(h/2)\), and the previously proved phase
complexity theorem gives \(h(T)=o(1)\), the final sum in (3) is
\(o(h^2)\). Thus each color supplies a positive-density set of
lags with same-color coincidence probability \(o(1)\).

One good lag of each color forces the stationary color frequencies
to be \(1/2+o(1)\). For example,
\(\Pr(T_0=T_d=0)\ge2\Pr(T_0=0)-1\).
The dense good set for either color is then a set of approximate
antiperiods in the sense of Section 1. It follows that typical
length-\(h\) phase blocks are \(o(h)\)-close to words of a bounded
period.

This is the needed bridge to the bounded-phase/arbitrary-row
inverse and its robust entropy gap. If the row coloring were also
far from every protected quadratic row law, all those bounded
period templates would have a uniform entropy gap. Counting their
small Hamming neighborhoods contradicts near-maximal entropy.
In this last entropy comparison, an exceptional block of
probability \(o(1)\) costs only \(o(1)\) after subtracting
\(\log(h/2)\): its unrestricted alphabet bound is \(h^h\), whose
ratio to \((h/2)^h\) is \(2^h\).

The conclusion concerns near-maximal sea entropy. It does not,
by itself, establish the stronger assertion that every arbitrary
full box with few three-corner rectangles is close to a protected
box.
