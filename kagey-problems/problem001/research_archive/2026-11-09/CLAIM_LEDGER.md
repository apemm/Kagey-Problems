## How to read this ledger

- Unless a path is given, a file name refers to `full_problem_push/` (relative to this folder, `2026-11-09/`).
- Statements are copied or tightly paraphrased from the opening statement of the proof note itself. Most notes were read through their statement and status paragraphs, not line by line. No proof was re-derived here.
- Audit notes were read at least through their verdict paragraph. Short ones were read in full.
- A row marked NOT READ means the proof note was not opened and the statement comes from a report.
- Audit status vocabulary:
  - AUDIT ON FILE: an independent audit note exists and names the proof note, or the proof note links it.
  - COMPUTER CERTIFICATE: the claim rests on a completed computation with a named checker or result file.
  - SELF-CHECKED ONLY: no separate audit note was found. If the note itself says it passed independent review, the flag column says so.
  - STATED AS UNAUDITED: the note or its report says the material is unrefereed, a sketch, or pending.
  - SUPERSEDED/CORRECTED: a later note replaced or corrected the claim.
- Kind vocabulary: THM (theorem with full proof), THM-EXT (full proof that imports a published external theorem), COND (theorem conditional on a stated hypothesis), LEAD (leading-order computation or heuristic), COMP (finite computation), CEX (counterexample or obstruction to a method).
