# Problem 131: publication notes

Updated September 24, 2026.

Paper A (`paperA/problem131.tex`) is the binary paper: uniform Bessel bounds, the crossings for
every bin, the Laguerre edge law, periodic boundaries, and the critical window. Paper B
(`paperB/problem131_multistate.tex`) is the d-direction paper and is still a draft. Paper B cites
Paper A as a preprint.

The crossing question itself is not new (Stepanyan et al. 2024 study the periodic case), and neither
are the exact law or the normal limit. The results that seem to be new are the uniform estimates
when p and the bin vary with N and what follows from them; see `literature_review.md`. Framed that
way, Paper A fits a probability or statistical-mechanics journal better than a note about Galton
boards. For Paper B the strongest results are the uniform near-vertex crossing for all A = o(b) and
the example where the crossing is approximated uniformly but the probabilities are not.

Before submitting:
- get the Wang and Yang (1995) paper for Paper B;
- have someone in the area read the proofs.

Length limits: Statistics & Probability Letters allows 6 journal pages (13 double-spaced pages),
so Paper A would need heavy cuts. For the Journal of Applied Probability, Cambridge's author
instructions say at most 25 printed pages, while the Applied Probability Trust's page says 1-30 pages
for JAP and 31 or more for AAP; check which applies before submitting.
