# The uniform local law (start of a separate paper)

These two files are the uniform local law for the persistent random walk, taken out of Paper A
before it was posted. They are the start of a separate paper and are kept here unchanged.

- `sec_llt.tex` was Section 6 of Paper A (the local law, its corollary, Remark 6.5 and Table 3).
- `app_llt.tex` was Appendix B of Paper A (the proof of the local law).

They do not build on their own. They cite results of Paper A (for example the exact law,
Theorem 2.1) by the labels of Paper A. The checks are `verify_uniform_llt.py` and
`verify_uniform_llt_cor5.py`, which stay in `paperA/`, and their output is in `paperA/data/`.
