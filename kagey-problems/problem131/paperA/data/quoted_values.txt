3. Section 5, mean domain length l_d = <N/(n_d + delta_{0,n_d})> at the periodic
   central crossing, for even N with 20 <= N <= 100.
   Here n_d is the number of domain walls of the whole periodic chain. At odds u
   the value n_d = 2r has weight proportional to C(N,2r) u^(2r), so
   l_d = sum_r C(N,2r) u^(2r) N/(2r + delta_{0,r}) / sum_r C(N,2r) u^(2r),
   evaluated at u = u_N^P.
   Method: u_N^P by bisection in exact rationals on the cyclic run polynomial
   (eq:periodicratio) with a = b = N/2, 200 steps from [0, 1], checked against
   root and periodic_ratio of verify_periodic_window.py to 1e-50. Then l_d in
   exact rationals at both ends of the final bracket and in mpmath with 60
   digits (the two ends agree to all printed digits). The exponents are
   least-squares slopes of log l_d and of log(N/log N) against log N over the
   41 even N from 20 to 100.
   N = 20:   u_N^P = 0.15148043263011737   l_d = 9.269333970525
   N = 100:  u_N^P = 0.04292803720573503   l_d = 31.210717974514
   Least-squares exponent of l_d, even N in [20, 100]:      0.7554709
   Least-squares exponent of N/log N, even N in [20, 100]:  0.7394336
   (over all N from 20 to 100 the second slope is 0.7399673)
   Quoted in the text: 9.27, 31.21, 0.755 and 0.74 (Section 5, after
