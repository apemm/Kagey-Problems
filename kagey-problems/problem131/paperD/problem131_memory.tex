\begin{abstract}
In the elephant random walk each step copies a uniformly chosen earlier step and
reverses it with probability $\varepsilon$. We let $\varepsilon\to0$ as the
number of steps $N$ grows, with $N\varepsilon$ of order $\log N$. Let $M_N$ be the
number of steps that disagree with the first step. We prove an explicit total
variation bound between the law of $M_N$ and a truncated Luria--Delbr\"uck law,
the compound Poisson law with jumps $d$ at rates $N\varepsilon/(d(d+1))$. A
Landau local limit theorem with an explicit rate follows. We then derive the
shape of the law of the position. Every mode lies at distance
$x\log x+\lambda_{\mathrm L}x+O(\sqrt{x\log x})$ from an end, where
$x=N\varepsilon$ and $\lambda_{\mathrm L}\approx-0.2228$ is the Landau mode. For
every $N$ and $\varepsilon<\frac12$ the second difference of the law changes sign
at most 4 times. For even $N$ the probabilities of the origin and of each end
cross exactly once, at $N\varepsilon=2\log N-\log\log N-\log16+o(1)$. There, for
every even $N\ge8$, the origin is the unique interior minimum and the law has
exactly 2 symmetric intervals of modes, by certified computations at finitely
many lengths. The sign theorem passes to the superdiffusive limit law at fixed
memory $a=1-2\varepsilon\in(\frac12,1)$. With a fair first step this law has at
most 2 modes for every $a$, and exactly 2 for $a\ge\frac{39}{40}$. By a
computer-assisted proof it is unimodal for $a\le0.668812860014597$ and
bimodal for $a\ge0.668812860014602$.
Unless its density is constant on some interval, this settles the fair-start case of a conjecture of
Gu\'erin, Laulin, Raschel and Simon.
\end{abstract}

\section*{A note on the use of AI}
I wrote the original draft and its mathematics. I used Claude Opus 5.5
(Anthropic) for revision and proof checking, and later used OpenAI Codex to
revise the exposition and check references. This included the abstract,
introduction, description of the verification and the arrangement of
the appendices and of the further results in the arXiv version.
Both models were used to expand literature search beyond what I had included, and all sources were read by me before citation if appropriate.
AI tools also generated the
Lean files and parts of the verification and certificate code, which I
reviewed against the paper. Section~\ref{sec:verify} distinguishes the formal proofs, their named
classical hypotheses and the separate numerical checks. I take responsibility
for all mathematical claims, proofs and computations.
