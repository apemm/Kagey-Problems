\ABSTRACT{We prove the conjecture of Gu\'erin, Laulin, Raschel and Simon
that the bimodal initial biases of the superdiffusive elephant random walk
form a possibly empty open interval centered at one half. Outside it,
including its endpoints, the limit density has a unique local maximum.
We pass finite sign bounds to the limit using weak convergence.
The fair density has exactly two inflection points when unimodal
and four when bimodal. A moment argument proves bimodality
for memory at least $0.79$ without computation.

We certify a unique fair-start transition
near $0.66881286$. At the transition the density has a quartic top.
The two modes separate on a square-root scale, while the bias interval
opens on a three-halves scale. Above the threshold, the bias boundary
is positive and continuously differentiable, with a variational formula
and a unique optimizer. Thus strongly biased starts are unimodal at
every fixed memory, although the boundary tends to zero as memory
tends to one.}

\section*{A note on the use of AI}
I wrote the original draft of this paper and its mathematics. I used Claude
Opus 5.5 (Anthropic, through Claude Code) for literature searches, citation
checks and referee-style reviews that informed the framing and abstract.
I read every source cited in the paper. I proposed and drafted the following
additions: Lemma~\ref{lem:analytic-density}; the strictness and
inflection-point clauses of Theorem~\ref{thm:two-modes}; the fourth-moment
extension of Theorem~\ref{thm:two-modes-near-one} to $a\ge4/5$; the Jensen
lower bound in Theorem~4.5(a) of the archived combined version; and the weaker
weak-convergence hypothesis in its Theorem~5.9(c).
I used GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI, through Codex) to revise the manuscript and to
revise the proofs of these additions. Proofs aside from the aforementioned were not altered by either model.
The associated Lean files in
\nolinkurl{kagey-problems/problem131/lean/PaperD} were originally written by me and edited using
GPT-6 Astra through Codex. Section~\ref{sec:verify} distinguishes the formal
proofs, their named classical hypotheses and the separate numerical checks.
I take responsibility for the claims, proofs and computations in the paper.
