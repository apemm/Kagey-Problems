Dear Editors,

Please consider "The elephant random walk with rare reversals: a Luria–Delbrück approximation and the shape of the law" for the Journal of Theoretical Probability.

The paper studies the elephant random walk when the reversal probability tends to 0 with the number of steps N, with N times that probability of order log N. The main result is an explicit total variation bound between the number of steps that disagree with the first step and a truncated Luria–Delbrück law. From it we derive a Landau local limit theorem with an explicit rate. The modes, the shape of the law and a crossing between the origin and the ends follow as consequences. We prove a unique crossing and locate it. For every even length at least 8 the law at the crossing has exactly 2 symmetric modal intervals and a unique interior minimum. A short weak-limit theorem connects the Landau law to the classical superdiffusive limit as the memory tends to 1.

The paper builds on the reversible fluctuation-assay model of Bokes, Hlubinová and Singh and on the Luria–Delbrück and elephant-walk literature. The contribution is the quantitative transfer to this walk, the error bounds, and the discrete shape and crossing results that follow from them. The preprint arXiv:2610.06935 treats a different walk, the persistent random walk. The article uses it only for a heuristic comparison.

The article is self-contained, and every proof it uses is in the article and its appendices. There is no supplementary material. A longer arXiv version adds the second-order center and crossing, the tail and further results for odd lengths. The verification section and the repository map separate the kernel-checked Lean results, their classical hypotheses and the certified computations.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for considering the paper.

Arjun Pemmasani
