Dear Editors,

Please consider “The center and the ends of the elephant random walk with rare reversals” for the Journal of Theoretical Probability.

The paper studies a reversal probability that decreases with the number of steps, so the walk makes about a logarithmic number of reversals. A truncated Luria–Delbrück approximation with an explicit total variation bound gives a Landau local limit with a rate and locates the modes. We prove a unique center–end crossing and, for every even length at least eight, exactly two symmetric modal intervals and a unique interior minimum at that crossing. A short weak-limit theorem connects the Landau law to the classical superdiffusive limit as memory tends to one.

The paper builds on the reversible fluctuation-assay model of Bokes, Hlubinová and Singh and the existing Luria–Delbrück and elephant-walk literature. The contribution is the quantitative transfer to this walk, the error bounds and the resulting discrete shape and crossing results. The related preprint arXiv:2610.06935 treats the persistent random walk; its crossing estimate is used for the comparison in the Online Resource, and the present walk has a different memory rule.

Every proof used by the main theorems is in the article and its appendices. Online Resource 1 contains the second-order center and crossing results, the tail theorem, the additional odd-length results and the likelihood bounds for the reversible fluctuation assay. Please send the resource for refereeing with the article. The verification section and repository map distinguish the kernel-checked Lean results, their classical hypotheses and the separate certified computations.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for considering the paper.

Arjun Pemmasani
