Dear Editors,

Please consider "Shape transitions and rare reversals in the elephant random walk" for the Journal of Theoretical Probability.

The paper connects finite-step shape to the superdiffusive limit through a bound on sign changes of second differences. With a fair first step, the limit density has either one mode or two symmetric modes. A characteristic-function bound excludes flat intervals. A certified computation determines which case holds outside an interval of length less than 3.3e-15 around memory 0.6688128600146. The proof does not establish a unique transition inside that interval. These results address the fair-start case of the bias-dependent conjecture of Guérin, Laulin, Raschel and Simon (Electron. J. Probab. 30 (2025), Remark 2.1(b)).

In the rare-reversal regime, an explicit total variation approximation by a truncated Luria–Delbrück law gives a Landau local limit theorem with a rate and locates every mode. Separate center estimates locate the unique crossing between the probabilities of a color tie and a unanimous outcome in the broadcasting representation. At that crossing, the sign theorem and finite certificates give two symmetric modal intervals and a unique interior minimum for every even length at least 8. The explicit constants make this all-length conclusion possible.

The paper distinguishes these results from the formal asymptotics of Bokes, Hlubinová and Singh, the weak Landau limits of Pakes and Bertoin, and the fixed-memory local-limit literature. The preprint arXiv:2610.06935 treats the persistent random walk and is used only for a heuristic comparison in the journal text.

The submission includes a review-only supplement, `threshold_proofs_review.pdf`, reproducing Section S6 of the longer version. It supplies all proofs behind the threshold certificate summarized in Appendix G. Referees therefore receive the complete argument with the submission. Sections S1–S5 contain additional results not used by the journal paper. The public code repository and verification section distinguish the recorded Lean kernel checks, their named classical hypotheses, and the separate interval computations. The new regularity, curvature and strengthened bounds are written proofs and are not yet formalized in Lean.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for considering the paper.

Arjun Pemmasani
