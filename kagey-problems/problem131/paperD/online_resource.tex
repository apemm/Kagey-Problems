This Online Resource accompanies ``The center and the ends of the
elephant random walk with rare reversals''.
Sections~\ref{sec:erw-second} to~\ref{or:odd} give the second-order,
tail and odd-$N$ results. Section~\ref{or:families} proves the family
estimates used for the mode bound, and Section~\ref{app:signs} proves
the second-difference results. These auxiliary proofs support the main
paper and are part of the material to be refereed.
Numbers prefixed S refer here; the other numbers refer to the paper.
We use its notation throughout.
