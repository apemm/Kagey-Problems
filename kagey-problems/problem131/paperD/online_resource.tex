\maketitle

This Online Resource belongs to the paper ``The center and the ends of the
elephant random walk with rare reversals'', submitted to the Journal of
Theoretical Probability. It holds results and proofs that
Theorems~\ref{thm:main-center} to~\ref{thm:main-shape} of the paper do not
use. Section~\ref{sec:erw-second} gives the center and the crossing to second
order, Section~\ref{or:tail} proves the tail of the law, and
Section~\ref{or:odd} proves parts (b), (c) and (d) of
Proposition~\ref{prop:odd}. Numbers with the prefix S refer to this Online
Resource, and all other numbers refer to the paper. We keep the notation and
the conventions of the paper.
