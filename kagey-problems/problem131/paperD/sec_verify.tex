Table~\ref{tab:verification} records how each claim is proved and what
the proof uses. Some Lean results are implications, so their hypotheses
still need proofs for this walk. In particular, Lean does not check
the transcendental enclosures at the transition. We compute those
separately with interval arithmetic.

We use Lean~4.33.1 with Mathlib pinned to \texttt{0df444a360ea}.
The declaration audit allows only \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
Lean checks the integer certificates by exact outward rounding,
without \texttt{native\_decide}. The total variation comparison,
martingale weak bridge, boundary continuity and critical expansions
have written proofs. Arb supplies the sharper local-limit constants
and the gap $\Delta_L=1/50$, whose inequalities are hypotheses of the
formal mode deduction.

For the transition, running \texttt{python -O} does not remove the
acceptance tests. We also check the two threshold endpoint signs by
direct evaluation at the real parameter values. This avoids the
parameter Taylor models, but still uses Arb and the same analytic
tail estimate.
