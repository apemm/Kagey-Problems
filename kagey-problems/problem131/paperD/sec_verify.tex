The formalization uses Lean~4.33.1 and Mathlib at \texttt{0df444a360ea}.
Its declaration audit allows only \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
Lean checks the integer certificates by exact outward rounding,
without \texttt{native\_decide}. We prove the total variation comparison,
martingale weak bridge, boundary continuity and critical expansions
in the text. The formal mode deduction takes the sharper local-limit
bounds and the gap $\Delta_L=1/50$ as hypotheses. Arb verifies these
inequalities separately.

The transition check retains its acceptance tests under
\texttt{python -O}. A second check evaluates the two threshold endpoint
signs directly at the real parameter values. It bypasses the parameter
Taylor models, though it still uses Arb and the same analytic tail bound.
