\section{Verification}\label{sec:verify}

{\sloppy
All computations are in Python, and the main ones were done by two independent
methods (for example, forward and backward recursions for the elephant center
agree to about $10^{-14}$ at $N=12800$). The proofs use
computation in three places only. First, some constants are values of
explicit sums and integrals, such as the 60-term sum in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:erw-bounds}(b), which we evaluated in 30-digit arithmetic
(\texttt{verify\_elephant\_crossing.py}). Second,
Lemma~\ref{lem:landau-mode} rests on enclosures of $f_L'$ at two points and
of $f_L''$ on the interval between them, computed in ball arithmetic with
rigorous error bounds. Third, Proposition~\ref{prop:dip-hyp}, which makes
Corollary~\ref{cor:dip} hold for every even $N\ge8$, rests for $N<1688$ on a
double-precision computation of $C_N/E_N$ at $\varepsilon=2/(N+2)$ whose
rounding error is bounded in Lemma~\ref{lem:rounding}. The last two
computations are in \texttt{certify\_elephant.py}, which also repeats the
third one in exact integer arithmetic for 20 values of $N$.
Conjecture~\ref{conj:convex}, the tables, the figures and the numbers quoted
outside the proofs rest on computation alone.

Some of the exact and finite statements are also proved in Lean~4, among
them Proposition~\ref{prop:mlr}, the value $C_N'(0)=4/(N+2)$ of
Proposition~\ref{prop:first-mutation}(b), Lemma~\ref{lem:unimodal},
Theorem~\ref{thm:dip}, Proposition~\ref{prop:aging-two} and the identities
behind Corollary~\ref{cor:golden}. The Lean files are in the folder
\nolinkurl{kagey-problems/problem131/lean/PaperD}. The file
\nolinkurl{MAP.md} in that folder lists each formalized statement with its
Lean name. The
asymptotic results and Theorem~\ref{thm:aging-d} were not formalized.

The scripts and the Lean files are in the repository named under ``Data
availability'' at the end of the paper, which also says how to rerun the
checks.
\par}
