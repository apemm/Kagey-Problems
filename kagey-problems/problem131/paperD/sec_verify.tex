\section{Computer assistance}\label{sec:verify}

Only the numerical location and critical derivative enclosures in
Appendix~\ref{app:threshold} use interval arithmetic. They use Arb~\cite{johansson2017}
through python-flint~0.8.0 at $1600$ bits, with independent endpoint
checks at $2000$ bits and refined constants at $2600$ bits through
python-flint~0.9.0. The supplement proves the truncation and tail
bounds. The \href{https://github.com/apemm/Kagey-Problems/tree/paperD-ejp-v8/kagey-problems/problem131/paperD}{tagged records}
give the inputs, acceptance tests and rerun commands.
Lean independently checks the finite sign arguments and analytic
threshold proof from the classical weak limit, as detailed in
\href{https://github.com/apemm/Kagey-Problems/blob/paperD-ejp-v8/kagey-problems/problem131/lean/PaperD/MAP.md}{the coverage map},
and is not needed to referee the written proofs.
