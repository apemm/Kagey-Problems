\section{Verification}\label{sec:verify}

The proofs use analytic arguments, finite interval certificates and Lean
checks. Table~\ref{tab:verification} separates their roles. A formal
deduction with stated mathematical inputs does not certify those inputs.
In particular, the transition's transcendental enclosures are numerical
proofs outside the Lean kernel.

The formal library uses Lean~4.33.1 and the pinned Mathlib revision
\texttt{0df444a360ea}. Its declaration audit admits only
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
Integer certificates are checked by exact outward rounding, without
\texttt{native\_decide}. The total variation comparison and the martingale
weak bridge remain written proofs. So do the critical asymptotic expansions.
The sharper local-limit constants and the gap $\kappa_L=1/50$ use Arb.
The formal mode deduction takes the gap inequalities as inputs.
The numerical transition checks retain their acceptance tests under
\texttt{python -O}. A separate real-parameter evaluation confirms the
threshold endpoint signs without the parameter Taylor models. It shares
Arb and the analytic tail argument, so it is an independent evaluation
path, not an independent arithmetic implementation.
