The \href{https://github.com/apemm/Kagey-Problems/tree/paperD-ejp-v4/kagey-problems/problem131/lean/PaperD}{shared formalization}
uses Lean~4.33.1 and Mathlib at \texttt{0df444a360ea}, with only
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}
allowed by the axiom audit. Besides finite recurrences and reflection
comparisons, it checks the analytic threshold proof's rational bounds,
strict covariance argument, unique-crossing deductions and Mellin
continuation. The actual $H_a$ series and its identification with the
randomized characteristic function and curvature remain written proofs.
The accompanying \nolinkurl{MAP.md} records the hypotheses of every
formal result and distinguishes current from archived results.
Lean does not replay the transcendental interval enclosures.
Boundary regularity and the critical expansions have written proofs.

The transition check retains its acceptance tests under
\texttt{python -O}. A second check evaluates the signs at the two bracket endpoints
directly, without the parameter Taylor models. It still uses Arb
and the same analytic tail bound.
