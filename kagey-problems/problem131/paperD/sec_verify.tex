uses Lean~4.33.1 and Mathlib at \texttt{0df444a360ea}.
Its audit checks $386$ modules and $24{,}005$ declarations, allowing
only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
The accompanying \nolinkurl{MAP.md} records each statement,
its hypotheses and whether it belongs to this paper or the archived
combined version. Lean checks finite recurrences, reflection
comparisons and several deductions from named analytic hypotheses.
It does not replay the transcendental interval enclosures.
Boundary regularity and the critical expansions have written proofs.
The map specifies exactly which statements the audit checks.

The transition check retains its acceptance tests under
\texttt{python -O}. A second check evaluates the signs at the two bracket endpoints
directly, without the parameter Taylor models. It still uses Arb
and the same analytic tail bound.
