\section{Data and formal verification records}\label{app:data}

The source, records and rerun commands are in the
\href{https://github.com/apemm/Kagey-Problems/tree/paperD-ejp-v1/kagey-problems/problem131/paperD}{tagged repository}.
Each numerical record includes source hashes, software versions,
precision and acceptance checks. These records and the Lean sources
are included in the submission archive.

\begin{itemize}
\item \nolinkurl{certify_elephant.py} checks the series bound giving
the constant $5200$ in Theorem~\ref{thm:erw-bounds} and the narrow
Landau-mode enclosure of Lemma~\ref{lem:landau-mode}.
\item \nolinkurl{certify_landau_gap.py} checks the global gap
$\Delta_L=1/50$, the Fourier decay bound $2|\theta|/5$ and the
local-limit constants $47$, $10.4$ and $2.7$ at $160$ bits with
python-flint~0.9.0. Its record is
\nolinkurl{data/landau_gap_certificate.json}.
\item \nolinkurl{certify_center.py} brackets the crossings for
$10\le N\le2000$ and checks their lower endpoints against $2/(N+2)$.
The rational witnesses in \nolinkurl{data/} give the finite input to
Proposition~\ref{prop:dip-hyp}.
\item \nolinkurl{certify_threshold.py}, with
\nolinkurl{threshold_taylor.py}, \nolinkurl{threshold_hankel.py} and
\nolinkurl{threshold_pointwise.py}, checks the parameter cover in
Table~\ref{tab:thr-runs}. Its nine records are
\nolinkurl{data/threshold_*.txt}. The mode \texttt{check} verifies that
their accepted intervals cover the stated ranges.
\item \nolinkurl{certify_transition.py} encloses the parameter derivative
of the central curvature and the fourth spatial derivative.
Its records \nolinkurl{data/transition_derivative_*} use $1100$
moments at $1600$ bits, Python~3.12.14 and python-flint~0.8.0.
\item \nolinkurl{check_critical_shape.py} evaluates the two threshold
endpoints with $1800$ moments at $2000$ bits and the conditional slope
with $1200$ moments, using python-flint~0.9.0. Its record is
\nolinkurl{data/critical_shape_check.json}.
\item \nolinkurl{certify_critical_constants.py} encloses $C_*$, $B_*$,
$H_*$ and the two derived coefficients on $I_*$ with $2000$ moments
at $2600$ bits, using python-flint~0.9.0. Its record is
\nolinkurl{data/critical_constants_certificate.json}.
Run both refined certificates with \texttt{python -O}.
\item \nolinkurl{verify_elephant_modes.py} produces the finite-length
illustrations in Remark~\ref{rem:modes-threshold}. They are not
certificates. The phase diagram is generated by
\nolinkurl{figures/make_phase_figure.py}, with data in
\nolinkurl{data/limit_phase_illustration.npz}.
\end{itemize}

The formal library is
\nolinkurl{kagey-problems/problem131/lean/PaperD}. Its
\nolinkurl{MAP.md} and \nolinkurl{coverage_tables.tex} identify each
formal statement and its explicit hypotheses. The full source inventory,
build log and axiom audit are in the
\href{https://github.com/apemm/Kagey-Problems/tree/paperD-ejp-v1/reports/paperD-ejp-2026-11-05}{verification record}.
The audit checks $376$ modules and $23{,}912$ declarations.
The classical inputs are those in Table~\ref{tab:verification}, together
with Hoeffding's hypergeometric bound. The transition interface assumes
the certified uniform Fourier envelopes. The written proofs establish
these inputs for the walk.
The module \nolinkurl{EJPGlobalModeGap.lean} proves the global-gap
and mode-stability deductions from the certified inequalities.
The compact localization and maximum-stability lemmas are in
\nolinkurl{EJPBiasBoundary.lean}. They assume quasiconcavity and the
strict endpoint comparisons. The density regularity and full boundary
continuity proof remain on paper. The sharper numerical constants
and explicit length threshold are not replayed in Lean.
