# Removed proofs of Paper D

`full_2026-09-26/` is Paper D as first written, at 50 pages, before it was cut to its current
length. It has the full proofs of the side results that are now remarks and the longer
explicit-constant proofs, kept for reference and in case a referee asks for them. The `.pdf` is the
compiled version, and the `.tex` sources are not meant to be compiled again from here.

`cuts_2026-10-15/` holds the four source files as they were before two side results were cut on
2026-10-15. These are Proposition 2.11 (the limit of k_2(N) - 2 log N), with its appendix
subsection and Lemma A.8, and Corollary 3.17 (the modes are O(x_N log^2 x_N) from the ends,
which Corollary 3.12 gives more sharply). The statement numbers in these files are the old ones.
