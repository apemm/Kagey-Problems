For $5\le N\le9$ the script is not run, and the check is done in Lean
(Section~\ref{sec:verify}) by a computation in exact integer arithmetic with
outward rounding, whose soundness Lean proves. Two rationals found by bisection
in exact rational arithmetic bracket $\varepsilon_N$. Lean proves this by Proposition~\ref{prop:mlr}
from enclosures of $P_N(\lceil N/2\rceil)$ and $E_N$ at the two rationals. Then
Lean encloses $P_N$ for every $\varepsilon$ between them at once. It checks that the
upper rational is below $\frac12$, that $P_N(0)<P_N(1)$, and that every index
$a^*\ge N/2$ that can be a mode either has an empty range
$2m^*+1\le a\le N-2m^*-1$ or has $2m^*+1\le N/2$ and $D_N(2m^*+1)>0$. By
Corollary~\ref{cor:one-point}, with $a_1=2m^*+1$, this gives the claim. At $N=5$
the only index that can be a mode is $a^*=4$, so the range is empty and the claim
holds trivially.
