\section{Numerical enclosures}\label{app:threshold}

The analytic proofs determine the transition and the critical exponents.
The computations below only locate the threshold and evaluate their constants.

\begin{corollary}[a certified location]\label{cor:threshold-bracket}
The unique threshold in Theorem~\ref{thm:threshold} satisfies
$z_-<a_*<z_+$, where
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 z_-&=1838418790980431/2748779069440000
      =0.668812860014597754\ldots,\\
 z_+&=45960469774511/68719476736000
      =0.668812860014601028\ldots.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The width of this interval is less than $3.3\cdot10^{-15}$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The endpoint evaluations give $g_{z_-}''(0)<0<g_{z_+}''(0)$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:threshold} identifies the zero between them.
Supplementary Sections~\ref{or:threshold} and~\ref{or:transition-unique}
prove the moment, truncation and Fourier-tail bounds used in these
interval evaluations. The endpoint calculation is independent of
parameter Taylor models, while both checks use Arb and the same
analytic tail bound.
\end{proof}
