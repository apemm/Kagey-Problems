Dear Editors,

Please consider “Face selection for slowly switching Markov chains” for the Electronic Journal of Probability.

For a finite chain with a fixed generator and a switching probability that decreases with its length, the paper compares the largest occupation-count probability on every admissible support and identifies the supports of the global modes in the logarithmic-switching regime. On irreducible competing supports, explicit constants locate the crossings beyond first order and resolve the complete graph's tie, including the intermediate supports selected by a nonuniform start or a weak field.

The manuscript incorporates the preprint arXiv:2610.09258, “Vertex crossings in a symmetric Markov multinomial model.” Its symmetric model is now an example within the general theory, and the results used here are stated and proved in the merged manuscript or its supplement.

The supplement contains complete proofs for the graph catalogue, the directed three-cycle, the higher-order Laplace estimate and the next-term expansion. Please send it for refereeing together with the main manuscript, since these proofs are part of the submission.

Thank you for considering the paper.

Arjun Pemmasani
