Dear Editors,

Please consider "What the occupation functional of a finite Markov chain determines: principal minors, modal supports and cycle trees" for Stochastic Processes and their Applications. The preprint is on arXiv as [arXiv number to be added at submission].

The paper studies an inverse problem. What does the occupation measure of a finite Markov chain determine about its generator? Kim and Landim (Stochastic Process. Appl. 189, 2025) showed that the Donsker and Varadhan functional determines a reversible generator, and that the 3-cycle and its reversal share it. We show that for every irreducible chain the functional determines exactly the principal minors of the negative generator (Theorem A). With the matrix theory of the principal-minor map, which we credit in detail, the generators with given minors form a finite set. It is the generator and its time reversal when there is no rank-one cut.

We then turn to a chain that makes about τ log N switches in N steps. The first-order support of its most likely vector of counts under killing at known rates determines exactly the same minors (Theorem B). We say plainly that this label is an artificial observable. At low temperature the functionals determine the depth of every face and the Freidlin and Wentzell cycle tree, with no reversibility or nondegeneracy, a set is a cycle exactly when its proper subsets are strictly shallower, and a threshold design fixed in advance reads the tree (Theorem C). We compare this with the Γ-expansions of Landim, of Bertini, Gabrielli and Landim, and of Kim and Landim.

The forward theory is in my paper "Occupation counts of rarely switching Markov chains" (arXiv:2610.09258v2), and the results used are stated in Section 2 with pointers. A design that reads a reversible generator from a minimal number of boundaries is kept in the arXiv version only.

The manuscript has 33 pages in the journal's preprint format, within the usual 30 to 40. Every stated result is proved in the article, and Section 6 lists what the scripts in the public repository check.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for considering the paper.

Arjun Pemmasani
