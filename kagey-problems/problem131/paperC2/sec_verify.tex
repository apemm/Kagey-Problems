\section{Computations and open questions}\label{sec:verify}

Every statement is proved in the text, with one kind of exception. The explicit numbers in the
example after Corollary~\ref{thm:nonreversible}, the moment $29(w_1-w_2)(w_3-w_4)/9673$ after
Theorem~\ref{thm:stationary-law}, the last 2 sentences of Example~\ref{ex:five-state}\ifarxivbuild\
and the numbers in Remark~\ref{rem:finite-size}\fi\ were computed, in rational arithmetic for the
examples, and the scripts below check them. The scripts also check other parts of the paper by assertions, with a
tolerance in floating point.

The script \texttt{verify\_reconstruction.py} checks Sections~\ref{sec:minors}
and~\ref{sec:modal}\ifarxivbuild\ and the reversible design of Section~\ref{sec:reversible}\fi.
On $64$ random generators with a rank-one cut it checks that the generator point of the
cut-transpose has the principal minors of $-Q$ (in $49$ of them it is neither $Q$ nor $Q^*$), and
it checks the four-state example after Corollary~\ref{thm:nonreversible} in rational arithmetic.
It checks the derivative used in the proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:perron-branch}, the three-state
identities of Remark~\ref{rem:three-states} symbolically, and on $200$ generators with 3 states
that every generator with the same minors is $Q$ or $Q^*$. The script
\texttt{verify\_exactlaw.py} checks Section~\ref{sec:exactlaw} in rational arithmetic. For
Example~\ref{ex:five-state} it checks the memoryless block, the 31 principal minors, the identity
$F_Q=F_{Q^{[B]}}$, the equality of $\Sigma_1$, $\Sigma_2$ and $\Sigma_3$, the laws of the counts for
$N\le6$ at $h=1/20$, the relation with the cut-transpose of Remark~\ref{rem:memoryless-cut}, the
start $e_1$ and the spectrum of $K^2$. For the four-state example it checks the stationary law of
$Q'$, both identities of Proposition~\ref{prop:gluing} and the first separating moment. It solves
$K'D^{-1}K'=KD^{-1}K$ for antisymmetric $K'$ with $K'\ones=0$ on 5 generators with 3 and 4 states,
where $K$ is the antisymmetric part of $DQ$, and finds only $\pm K$, as
Theorem~\ref{thm:second-moments}(i) asserts, since $\Sigma_2=2\operatorname{sym}(DQ)D^{-1}\operatorname{sym}(DQ)+2KD^{-1}K$
when $\pi Q=0$. It also checks the moment formula and the case $N=2$ of
Remark~\ref{rem:one-N}.\ifarxivbuild\ It verifies the inversion
formula of Theorem~\ref{thm:reversible-reconstruction}(b) and the family of
Remark~\ref{rem:partial-data}, recovers $Q$ from the strict winners on $40$ random reversible
chains with 4 states, with Lemma~\ref{lem:suppression} checked on a grid of every design, and
checks the constant $20/3+28/9<10$ in the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:stability} and the bounds
$\phi_*$ and $g_*$ on $2000$ chains. The script \texttt{verify\_finite\_horizon.py} computes the
entry times of Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-horizon} on a path with 3 states for $N=10^3$ to $10^6$
from the exact run expansion of~\cite[Lemma~2.3]{paperC1}, and the numbers of
Remark~\ref{rem:finite-size}. The script \texttt{verify\_threshold.py} computes the least $N$ after
Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-horizon} for 2 chains and $b=2^{-1},\dots,2^{-8}$.\fi\ The script
\texttt{verify\_cycles.py} redoes Examples~\ref{ex:four-state} and~\ref{ex:branch-switch} in exact
arithmetic. The script \texttt{verify\_attraction.py}, with the module \texttt{attraction\_core.py},
tests Theorem~\ref{thm:attraction} on $11$ cost matrices and $138$ proper faces at $t=10^{-32}$ and
$10^{-48}$, with the exact finite-$\eta$ scores. Every cycle has the grid threshold $D(G)-1/4$, no
noncycle exceeds it, and $\max_{G\subseteq F}\vartheta(G)$ gives $D(F)$ on every face.\ifarxivbuild\else\
The repository also holds the scripts for the reversible design of the arXiv version.\fi

Some finite and algebraic steps are also proved in Lean~4 with Mathlib, in
\nolinkurl{lean/PaperC} of the same repository. The module \texttt{PrincipalMinors} proves the
multiaffine expansion of $R_F$. The module \texttt{GeneratorFiber} proves the normalization (N),
the fact that a diagonal similarity keeping an irreducible Metzler matrix Metzler has entries of
one sign, and Corollary~\ref{thm:nonreversible}(d) for positive rates. The module
\texttt{CriticalWindow} proves the determinants, adjugates and prefactors of
Example~\ref{ex:four-state}, and \texttt{DVRestriction} the restriction identity of
Proposition~\ref{prop:variational}(a). Mathlib has no Perron and Frobenius theorem for irreducible
Metzler matrices, so the positive null vector in (N) is a hypothesis there. The library builds
with \texttt{lake build PaperC}, and every theorem in it depends only on the axioms
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}. The build log is
\nolinkurl{lean/build-log.txt}.

\emph{Open questions.} For the exact law, Conjectures~\ref{conj:fiber} and~\ref{conj:balanced}
are open. So is whether the stationary second moments of all orders always have the same fiber
as the stationary law, and what the law of the counts at one pair $(N,T)$ determines. The finite-horizon label $\mathcal W_N$ of Section~\ref{sec:observation}
may determine more than $W_Q$. The crossing equation of~\cite[Theorem~5.8]{paperC1} for the
weighted law would read the amplitudes of Proposition~\ref{prop:amplitudes} off the positions of
the changes of $\mathcal W_N$, and so separate the fiber of Corollary~\ref{thm:nonreversible} by
finite-horizon labels, but this is not proved. We do not know whether a single irreducible face is
always traced against a singleton rather than a larger subface in the exposure lemma. At low
temperature it is open whether some calibrated field separates $a_{124}$ from $a_{12}$ in
Example~\ref{ex:four-state}, and a version of Theorem~\ref{thm:attraction} with $\eta$ tied to $N$
is open.
