\section{Verification}\label{sec:verify}

No proof uses a computed number. The computations check the exact
identities, produce Figure~\ref{fig:atlas} and Table~\ref{tab:antimode}, and
test the limit theorems at moderate $N$. Except for the identities, all of
them use the zeta law $f_\sgn(n)=n^{-5/2}/\zeta(5/2)$ for both signs, so
$\alpha_\pm=3/2$, $p=1/2$ and $s=3.894745$, with a fresh plus start unless we say otherwise.

\emph{The exact law.} For a fresh plus start let $a_t(k)$ and $b_t(k)$ be the
probabilities that a plus run, respectively a minus run, starts at slot $t+1$
and the first $t$ slots contain exactly $k$ plus slots. So
$a_t(k)=\rho^{(\plus)}_{t-k}(k)$, $a_0(0)=1$ and $b_0(0)=0$. Since the runs
alternate, with $a_t(k)=b_t(k)=0$ for $k<0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:run-recursion}
 b_t(k)=\sum_{\ell=1}^{t}a_{t-\ell}(k-\ell)\,f_\plus(\ell),\qquad
 a_t(k)=\sum_{\ell=1}^{t}b_{t-\ell}(k)\,f_\minus(\ell)\qquad(t\ge1).
\end{equation}
By~\eqref{eq:rho-rep} the identity~\eqref{eq:final-explicit} of
Proposition~\ref{prop:final-run} then reads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:run-sum}
 P_N(N-j)=\sum_{t=j}^{N-1}a_t(t-j)\,T_\plus(N-t)+\sum_{m=1}^{j}b_{N-m}(N-j)\,T_\minus(m).
\end{equation}
The recursion gives the $O(N^2)$ numbers $a_t(k)$ and $b_t(k)$ with $t\le N-1$
in $O(N^3)$ operations, and~\eqref{eq:run-sum} then gives the whole row in
$O(N^2)$ more. The script \nolinkurl{certify_atlas.py} runs it in double
precision. Every entry is one dot product of earlier entries with nonnegative
terms. So every computed bin has relative error at most
$\varepsilon_N=(1+\gamma_{N+2})^{N+2}-1$, with $\gamma_n=nu/(1-nu)$ and
$u=2^{-53}$, whatever the order of summation. This bound is
$4.0\cdot10^{-11}$ at $N=600$ and $2.8\cdot10^{-9}$ at $N=5000$. At $N=600$ we
also run the recursion in $128$-bit ball arithmetic. The double-precision row
lies in the balls widened by $\varepsilon_{600}$, with a largest relative
deviation of $5.9\cdot10^{-15}$.

\emph{The figure.} The script \nolinkurl{figures/make_atlas_figure.py} draws
Figure~\ref{fig:atlas} from the law at $N=5000$, with $h_N$ obtained by
inverting its characteristic function. The horizontal axis of panel (a) is
linear on $[250,4750]$ and stretched on the last $250$ bins at each end, which
makes the two kinks. The shaded bands have width $B_N=74$ and mark the
junctions between the pieces. The largest relative error of the bulk on
$|k-pN|\le3B_N$ is $0.071$. That of the big-jump kernel where
$|k-pN|\ge4B_N$ and $\min(k,N-k)\ge2B_N$ is $0.38$, attained at the junction
with the bulk, and that of the boundary kernel on $1\le\min(k,N-k)\le B_N$ is
$0.094$. The atom is exact.

\emph{The antimode.} Table~\ref{tab:antimode} compares the leading law and
the refined prediction of Theorem~\ref{thm:antimode} with the exact law. The script
\nolinkurl{certify_antimode.py} finds the minimizer of $P_N$ on $[pN,N]$. It is
certified when every other bin there exceeds the minimum by a relative margin
above $2\varepsilon_N/(1-\varepsilon_N)$, which is at most $1.5\cdot10^{-8}$.
The smallest margins are $2.6\cdot10^{-4}$, $2.3\cdot10^{-4}$,
$1.7\cdot10^{-5}$, $3.1\cdot10^{-5}$ and $1.1\cdot10^{-5}$ for the five rows.

\emph{The coefficient.} The script \nolinkurl{verify_coefficient.py} checks
that the two forms of~\eqref{eq:Cj} agree for $j\le6000$ and every entry of
Table~\ref{tab:antimode}. The limit $2\alpha_\plus s/\mu_\minus^2$ of $C(j)/j$
in Theorem~\ref{thm:second-order} is $3.0811$, while $C(j)/j$ equals $4.968$,
$4.430$, $4.041$, $3.762$, $3.563$, $3.422$ and $3.323$ at
$j=5,10,20,40,80,160,320$. At $j=5$, $10$, $20$ and $40$ the script compares
$C(j)/j$ with $[P_N(N-j)/T_\plus(N)-u_\minus(j)]N/j$ for $N=1200$, $2400$ and
$4800$. The distance halves each time $N$ doubles, from $0.090$ to $0.022$ at
$j=5$ and from $0.592$ to $0.135$ at $j=40$. The ratio of the two sides
of~\eqref{eq:second-renewal} is $0.978$, $0.995$ and $0.999$ at $j=40$, $160$
and $640$. The script also checks the constant $\mathcal C(\varphi)$ and the
bound $0.9513\ldots$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:mode}(d).

\emph{The preparation and the big-jump zone.} The script
\nolinkurl{verify_preparation.py} computes the law for a general preparation
by the recursion with the first run inserted, and checks it against
Proposition~\ref{prop:first-run}. It takes the fresh plus start, the stationary
start and a start with $Q_\plus(n)=n^{-0.3}/2$, for which
$\rho=0.3<\alpha_\plus-1$. For $j\in\{1,2,3,5,10,20\}$ the largest relative
deviation of $P_N(N-j)$ from the exact defect form of
Theorem~\ref{thm:preparation}(d) falls from $0.12$ at $N=1200$ to $0.059$ at
$N=2400$ for the fresh start, from $0.057$ to $0.028$ for the stationary start
and from $0.013$ to $0.006$ for the third start. For the stationary start
the ratio of $P_N(N-j)/T_\plus(N)$ to $\kappa^{\mathrm{st}}_\plus(j)$ in
Corollary~\ref{cor:stationary}(c) is $1.0007$, $1.0031$ and $1.0098$ at $j=1$,
$2$ and $5$ for $N=2400$, and each deviation is about half of its value at
$N=1200$. The ratios in the big-jump zone are printed without a test, since
Theorem~\ref{thm:preparation}(c) is a double limit. The script
\nolinkurl{verify_bigjump.py} checks Remark~\ref{rem:no-single-formula}. The
limit of $P_N(k)/G_N(k)$ at $k=\lceil pN+KB_N\rceil$ is $0.31$, $1.68$ and
$1.09$ at $K=1$, $4$ and $16$. The exact ratio is $0.354$, $1.482$ and $1.036$
at $N=1200$, and $0.337$, $1.538$ and $1.059$ at $N=3000$. These move toward
the limits, and at $N=3000$ they are still about $8$ percent off at $K=1$ and $K=4$.

\emph{The identities.} The script \nolinkurl{verify_identities.py} checks
Propositions~\ref{prop:fresh-identity}, \ref{prop:first-run}
and~\ref{prop:final-run}, the latter in both
forms~\eqref{eq:final-explicit} and~\eqref{eq:run-sum} with the
recursion~\eqref{eq:run-recursion}, Lemmas~\ref{lem:green}
and~\ref{lem:counting}, \eqref{eq:kernel-sums}, \eqref{eq:kappa},
\eqref{eq:cover} to~\eqref{eq:conv-v} and the inversion of
Theorem~\ref{thm:inverse} in exact rational arithmetic. It uses two pairs of
run laws with finite support and a preparation with atoms of both signs,
some beyond the horizon. The law is computed as a sum over all $2^N$ sign
strings for $N\le12$, and independently by a chain that moves slot by slot
with the hazard $f_\sgn(a)/T_\sgn(a)$ of the current run. At $N=1200$ and
$j\le40$ the script \nolinkurl{verify_coefficient.py} matches the two forms of
Proposition~\ref{prop:final-run} in double precision to $2.2\cdot10^{-15}$.

\emph{The Lean library.} The library \nolinkurl{PaperF} in
\nolinkurl{kagey-problems/problem131/lean} has $7$ modules, in which a walk
of $N$ slots is a word of signs. The Lean kernel checks the fresh identities
(\texttt{fresh\_plus}, \texttt{fresh\_minus}), the kernel sums
(\texttt{kernel\_sum\_u}, \texttt{kernel\_sum\_q},
\texttt{kernel\_sum\_q\_succ}), Proposition~\ref{prop:first-run}
(\texttt{first\_run}), Proposition~\ref{prop:final-run} in the
form~\eqref{eq:run-sum} with the recursion~\eqref{eq:run-recursion}
(\texttt{final\_run}, \texttt{A\_succ}, \texttt{B\_succ}),
Lemma~\ref{lem:green} as a convergent series (\texttt{green\_plus},
\texttt{green\_minus}), the first part of Lemma~\ref{lem:counting}
(\texttt{counting}) and the inversion of Theorem~\ref{thm:inverse}(a)
(\texttt{renewal\_inverse}, \texttt{renewal\_injective}). No module uses
\texttt{sorry} or \texttt{native\_decide}, and \texttt{\#print axioms}
reports only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and
\texttt{Quot.sound}. The bound of Lemma~\ref{lem:counting},
\eqref{eq:kappa}, the limits in Theorem~\ref{thm:inverse} and every
asymptotic statement are not formalized. The file
\nolinkurl{lean/PaperF/MAP.md} maps the labels to the Lean names.

\emph{Software.} The scripts, their outputs with the hash of each script
and the date, and the Lean files are in the folder
\nolinkurl{kagey-problems/problem131} of
\url{https://github.com/apemm/Kagey-Problems}. In \nolinkurl{paperF},
\texttt{python -I verify\_all.py} runs the four \texttt{verify} scripts in

\texttt{certify} scripts first, in about two minutes. In \nolinkurl{lean},
\texttt{lake exe cache get} and \texttt{lake build PaperF} build the
library. We used Python~3.14.3, numpy~2.4.2, scipy~1.17.1, mpmath~1.3.0,
python-flint~0.9.0 (FLINT~3.6.0, with Arb), matplotlib~3.10.8, Lean~4.33.1
and Mathlib~v4.33.1 (commit \texttt{0df444a360ea}).
