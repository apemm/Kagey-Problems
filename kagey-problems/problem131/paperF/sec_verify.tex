\section{Verification}\label{sec:verify}

{\sloppy
All computations are in Python. The values of $R_N$ in Table~\ref{tab:heavy}
were computed in two ways, both from Proposition~\ref{prop:tworenewal},
namely by its formula and by a spectral method that reads each factor off as
a Taylor coefficient of a generating function. For small $N$ they were also
checked against a recursion on the first run and a sum over all run
compositions, which do not use Proposition~\ref{prop:tworenewal}. The aging probabilities, and with them the crossing
$c_{200}$, were computed both by a forward recursion and from transfer
matrices at roots of unity. No proof in this paper rests on a computation. The tables,
the figures and the numbers quoted outside the proofs rest on computation
alone. The script \texttt{verify\_heavy\_runs.py} recomputes the numbers of
Section~\ref{sec:heavy} and Appendix~\ref{app:heavy}, the script \texttt{verify\_aging.py}
those of Section~\ref{sec:aging} and Appendix~\ref{app:aging}, and
\texttt{verify\_aging\_urn.py} checks Proposition~\ref{prop:aging-urn} in exact arithmetic for
two, three and four states. The numbers in the caption of
Figure~\ref{fig:aging} come from \texttt{figures/make\_aging\_figure.py}.

Some of the exact and finite statements are also proved in Lean~4, namely
Proposition~\ref{prop:aging-two}, Proposition~\ref{prop:aging-fair},
Proposition~\ref{prop:aging-urn} for two states, the finite part of
Proposition~\ref{prop:edge} and the identities behind
Corollary~\ref{cor:golden}. The Lean files are in
the folder \nolinkurl{kagey-problems/problem131/lean/PaperD}. The file
\nolinkurl{MAP.md} in that folder lists each formalized statement with its
Lean name. The folder is shared with an earlier version of this work, and
\nolinkurl{MAP.md} uses its numbering of statements and equations, in which
Sections~\ref{sec:heavy} and~\ref{sec:aging} were Sections~4 and~5 and
Appendix~\ref{app:aging} was Appendix~E. The statements did not all keep their place.
Corollary~4.5, Remark~4.6, Theorems~4.7 and~4.8 and Table~3 there are
Corollary~\ref{cor:golden}, Remark~\ref{rem:crossover}, Theorems~\ref{thm:alpha2}
and~\ref{thm:lamperti} and Table~\ref{tab:outcomes} here, and Propositions~5.1, 5.4
and~5.6, Lemma~E.1 and Proposition~E.2 there are Propositions~\ref{prop:aging-two},
\ref{prop:aging-urn} and~\ref{prop:aging-fair}, Lemma~\ref{lem:D-average} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:D-bracket} here. Not formalized are
Proposition~\ref{prop:tworenewal}, Theorem~\ref{thm:aging-d},
Proposition~\ref{prop:aging-urn} for $d\ge3$ and the asymptotic results.

The scripts and the Lean files are in the repository named under ``Data and
code availability'' at the end of the paper, which also says how to rerun the
checks.
\par}
