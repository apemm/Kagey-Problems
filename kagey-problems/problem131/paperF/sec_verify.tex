\section{Computations}\label{sec:verify}

No proof uses a computed number. The computations check the exact identities,
produce Figure~\ref{fig:atlas} and Table~\ref{tab:antimode}, and test the limit
theorems at moderate $N$. Except for the identities they use the zeta law
$f_\sgn(n)=n^{-5/2}/\zeta(5/2)$ for both signs, so $\alpha_\pm=3/2$, $p=1/2$ and
$s=3.894745$, with a fresh plus start unless we say otherwise.

For a fresh plus start let $a_t(k)$ and $b_t(k)$ be the probabilities that a plus
run, respectively a minus run, starts at step $t+1$ and the first $t$ steps contain
exactly $k$ plus steps. So $a_t(k)=\rho^{(\plus)}_{t-k}(k)$, $a_0(0)=1$ and $b_0(0)=0$.
Since the runs alternate, with $a_t(k)=b_t(k)=0$ for $k<0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:run-recursion}
 b_t(k)=\sum_{\ell=1}^{t}a_{t-\ell}(k-\ell)\,f_\plus(\ell),\qquad
 a_t(k)=\sum_{\ell=1}^{t}b_{t-\ell}(k)\,f_\minus(\ell)\qquad(t\ge1),
\end{equation}
and by~\eqref{eq:rho-rep} the identity~\eqref{eq:final-explicit} reads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:run-sum}
 P_N(N-j)=\sum_{t=j}^{N-1}a_t(t-j)\,T_\plus(N-t)+\sum_{m=1}^{j}b_{N-m}(N-j)\,T_\minus(m).
\end{equation}
This gives the whole law in $O(N^3)$ operations. Every entry is a dot product with
nonnegative terms, so in double precision every computed value has relative error
at most $(1+\gamma_{N+2})^{N+2}-1$ with $\gamma_n=nu/(1-nu)$ and $u=2^{-53}$, which
is $2.8\cdot10^{-9}$ at $N=5000$. At $N=600$ a run in ball arithmetic confirms the
double-precision law. In Figure~\ref{fig:atlas}, with $B_N=74$, the largest
relative error of the bulk on $|k-pN|\le3B_N$ is $0.071$, that of the big-jump
kernel where $|k-pN|\ge4B_N$ and $\min(k,N-k)\ge2B_N$ is $0.38$, attained at the
junction with the bulk, and that of the boundary kernel on $1\le\min(k,N-k)\le B_N$
is $0.094$. At $N=1200$ and $3000$ the ratio $P_N(k)/G_N(k)$ at
$k=\lceil pN+KB_N\rceil$ is $0.354$, $1.482$, $1.036$ and $0.337$, $1.538$, $1.059$
for $K=1$, $4$, $16$, against the limits $0.31$, $1.68$, $1.09$ of
Remark~\ref{rem:no-single-formula}. The minimizers in Table~\ref{tab:antimode}
are certified against the rounding bound. For the stationary initial run the ratio
of $P_N(N-j)/T_\plus(N)$ to $\kappa^{\mathrm{st}}_\plus(j)$ is $1.0007$, $1.0031$
and $1.0098$ at $j=1$, $2$ and $5$ for $N=2400$, about half its deviation at
$N=1200$. For Section~\ref{sec:extrema}, the ratio $d_j\mu_\minus^2/T_\minus(j)$ of
Lemma~\ref{lem:diff-renewal} is $-0.963$, $-0.996$ and $-0.9996$ at $j=10$, $100$ and
$1000$. The error term of Theorem~\ref{thm:first-diff} for a fresh plus start at $j=20$ is
$0.73$ at $N=1000$ and $0.23$ at $N=3000$, so at this $j$ it decays like $1/N$. For the fair start the
law has exactly three modes, at $k=1$, $N/2$ and $N-1$, for $N=400$ and $1000$. For the start
of Corollary~\ref{cor:four-modes} with $d_0=5$ it has exactly four modes at $N=1000$, at
$k=1$, $499$, $995$ and $1000$. These modes are certified, since every relative difference
of neighbouring values exceeds twice the rounding bound. The exact identities of Section~\ref{sec:setting}, the renewal
identities of Section~\ref{sec:second-order} and~\eqref{eq:renewal-inverse} are
checked in exact rational arithmetic against a sum over all sign strings for
$N\le12$. The identities of Section~\ref{sec:identities}, except the bound in
Lemma~\ref{lem:counting}, are also proved in the Lean library \nolinkurl{PaperF}, whose file \nolinkurl{MAP.md} maps the labels
to the Lean names.

The scripts, their outputs with the hash of each script, and the Lean files are in
the folder \nolinkurl{kagey-problems/problem131} of
\url{https://github.com/apemm/Kagey-Problems}. In \nolinkurl{paperF} the command
\texttt{python -I verify\_all.py} runs the checks, and \nolinkurl{README.md} lists
the software versions and what each script tests.
